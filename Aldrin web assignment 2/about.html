About the department

Who we are

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at FISAT, Angamaly, Kerala, offers a four-year B.Tech programme affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).

Overview

A department built on curiosity

The department offers a four-year B.Tech programme affiliated to KTU. Students learn through projects, labs and mentoring, and are encouraged to question, experiment and collaborate.

Admissions open for 2026–27.
